02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE SCIENCE

Curiosity, at
every scale.

I'm Manisha Muduli, a Photonics Scientist at QC82 working on single-photon avalanche diodes (SPADs) and photonic sensing technologies.

My work is driven by a simple question: how can cutting-edge materials and fabrication techniques unlock new possibilities for electronic and photonic systems?

My path began with a B.Tech. in Nanotechnology at Amity University, followed by an M.S.E. in Nanotechnology at the University of Pennsylvania. I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at The Ohio State University in August 2026, researching infrared sensors, avalanche photodiodes, and III–V/silicon integration with Prof. Sanjay Krishna.

That work connected device design, microfabrication, and electrical and optical characterization—a foundation I now bring to InGaAs-based SPAD process development at QC82. Along the way, I explored 300 mm wafer bonding at Intel and membrane-transfer approaches through visiting research at Tyndall and Wisconsin–Madison.

Outside the lab, I find balance outdoors—skiing, hiking, and spending time in nature. I have a deep appreciation for the natural world and the creatures we share it with.
